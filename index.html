Our Story

Born from Passion,
Served with Love

Winnie's Cuisine and Events was born from a simple belief: great food should feel like a warm embrace. We opened in December 2025 at Nwabude Mall, Centenary City Estate, bringing Enugu a dining experience that's genuinely world-class yet unmistakably Nigerian.

Every plate is a celebration of local ingredients, time-honoured Nigerian traditions, and international technique. Whether you're here for a quiet lunch or the most important event of your life, we believe every guest deserves to feel like royalty.

At Winnie's, hospitality is not a service. It is our language. We listen, we personalise, and we deliver memories that linger long after the last course.
